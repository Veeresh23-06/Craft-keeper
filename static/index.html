Some knowledge only lives in people.

Across our communities, the most precious traditional skills—from heirloom kitchen recipes and herbal preparations to handloom weaving and earthen pottery—exist entirely in people's voices, hands, and memories.

When these skills are recorded, they usually remain trapped in hour-long phone videos or scattered audio notes, making them difficult for families, schools, and small community archives to preserve or pass on.

How Craft Keeper works

Four transparent steps from spoken explanation to verified archive.

1

Upload a video

A short clip (2 to 3 minutes) of someone explaining a skill aloud.

2

The AI listens & watches

It writes an exact transcript and extracts timestamps without inventing facts.

3

You get a structured guide

Ingredients, numbered steps with oral tips, and flagged uncertainties.

4

You review and share

A human practitioner verifies and edits, then downloads Markdown or JSON.

What you get

Every guide is structured, attributed, and ready for human verification.

Traditional Mysuru Paak

Knowledge shared by Ajji (Grandmother), Karnataka Confectioners, Mysuru
Kannada Recipe

Ingredients & Materials

  • Besan: 1 cup (sieved twice)
  • Desi Ghee: 2 cups (kept warm)
  • Sugar: 2 cups cane sugar
  • Water: 1/2 cup

Steps & Spoken Wisdom

1. [0:08] Sieve flour and fold in warm melted ghee.
Tip: "Rubbing warm ghee into dry flour stops lumps from ever forming."
2. [0:21] Boil sugar syrup to one-string consistency.
Tip: "Test between fingers—it must pull a continuous thin string."
Please check these (Model Uncertainties)
• Flame setting: Audio obscured by bubbling at 0:42. Verify burner intensity.
• Resting duration: Speaker said 'few minutes' without exact count.

Built to be honest

Core ethical guidelines baked directly into the code and user experience.

Consent first

You explicitly verify permission before anything is processed. Anonymity is always an option.

No guessing

The AI must not invent steps or halluncinate measurements. Unclear moments are prominently flagged.

Transparent provenance

A "How this was made" panel tells you the exact model path, frame count, and processing date.

Knowledge holder credit

Artisans and community knowledge holders are explicitly attributed in all exported Markdown and JSON files.

Not just recipes

Craft Keeper captures any living skill someone explains aloud on video.

Artisan crafts

Handloom weaving, pottery, bamboo basketry, wood carving, and natural dye mixing.

Village & farming skills

Seed saving, grafting, compost preparation, rainwater harvesting, and traditional storage.

Festivals & folk arts

Rangoli and kolam geometry, flower garlands, ceremonial confections, and folk instrumentation.

School & community archives

Student oral history interviews, community craft handouts, and public library local heritage guides.

Frequently asked questions

Honest, direct answers about capabilities and limits.

A free, open-source tool that turns short videos (2 to 3 minutes) of traditional crafts, recipes, and artisan skills into structured, editable guides with timestamps, oral tips, and safety notes.
No. While heirloom family recipes are a core use case, it works for any skill someone demonstrates and explains aloud on video—including weaving, pottery, woodworking, seed saving, and folk arts.
In local mode (Ollama + Whisper), your video stays entirely on your computer with zero network access. In hosted mode (Google AI Studio Free Tier), audio or transcripts are sent to the model provider under their official API terms. Craft Keeper displays a clear badge indicating which mode is active.
Yes. Background kitchen noise or unclear speech can cause errors. That is why every output is prominently marked as a draft, uncertain measurements are flagged in an amber checklist, and a human practitioner must review before anything is published.
English and standard Hindi work reliably with Whisper base. Kannada, Tamil, and regional dialects are currently experimental and undergoing testing. We do not claim untested accuracy metrics.
Only in local mode, and only after the Whisper and Gemma models have been downloaded to your machine. The default Mock mode also runs completely offline without any internet connection.
The person demonstrating the skill is credited by name in the header of the exported Markdown and JSON files ("Knowledge shared by [name], [community], [place]"). If they prefer privacy, an "Anonymous" toggle is provided.
Yes, Craft Keeper is 100% free under the Apache-2.0 license. You can contribute sample heritage guides, benchmark datasets, language translations, or code via our GitHub repository.

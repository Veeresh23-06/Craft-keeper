# Responsible AI & Ethical Stewardship in Craft Keeper

Craft Keeper is built to preserve living audiovisual heritage—traditional knowledge, recipes, and artisanal techniques passed down through oral and visual demonstration. Because traditional knowledge is intimately tied to individual makers, lineages, and cultural communities, responsible AI practices and rigorous transparency are core architectural requirements, not afterthoughts.

---

## 1. Consent and Agency
- **Mandatory Permission**: The app enforces an explicit consent checkbox before generation can proceed:
  > *"I have permission to use this video, and the person in it agrees."*
- **Right to Anonymity**: Traditional knowledge holders have the absolute right to control how their identity is displayed. Craft Keeper provides a *"Keep knowledge holder anonymous"* toggle that automatically credits the demonstration to `"Anonymous"` across all generated outputs, JSON schemas, and exported Markdown documents.
- **Credit and Cultural Provenance**: When artisans choose to be credited, Craft Keeper prominently preserves their name, community or guild, geographic place of practice, and the cultural occasion or context.

---

## 2. Mandatory Human Review Banner
AI-generated outputs are first drafts, never authoritative final records. Every screen, JSON export, and Markdown document produced by Craft Keeper bears the prominent notice:

> **⚠️ AI-Generated Draft:** This guide was generated by AI from video and audio demonstration. A human practitioner, elder, or community archivist must review and verify all measurements, techniques, and instructions before it is shared or practiced.

---

## 3. Highlighting Model Uncertainty
Craft Keeper enforces strict grounding rules:
- Models are instructed never to invent ingredients, tools, quantities, or facts.
- Anything muffled, occluded, ambiguous, or partially heard is explicitly flagged under the **`uncertain_items`** section (`what` and `reason`).
- In the UI and exports, uncertain items are highlighted in a prominent **"Please Check These"** callout box to direct human attention directly to potential errors.

---

## 4. Known Technical Limitations & Honest Accuracy Reporting
- **Regional Indian Languages & Dialects (Untested)**: Speech recognition accuracy on regional languages (including Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi) and regional English accents has **not been formally benchmarked** by this project. In empirical testing on regional culinary terminology, Whisper `base` phonetically transcribed *"kadai"* as *"cadet"* and *"sieved"* as *"sived"*. Human verification is essential.
- **Acoustic Noise**: Kitchens and craft workshops frequently contain ambient sounds (sizzling oil, hammering, background speech) that can degrade audio transcription fidelity.
- **Sensory Cues**: Traditional crafts rely heavily on touch, smell, and visual cues (e.g., "one-string sugar consistency", "honeycomb frothing"). The model relies on the maker's spoken tips and visual frames, but cannot experience physical sensations.

---

## 5. Data Privacy and Provider Terms

| Backend Mode | Network Transmission | Data Privacy Guarantee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`mock`** (Default) | **Zero (100% Offline)** | No data leaves your machine; uses pre-packaged synthetic sample data. |
| **`whisper+gemma-local`** | **Zero (100% Offline)** | Audio and video frames are processed entirely on your local CPU/GPU using open-source models (`faster-whisper` and local Gemma via Ollama). |
| **`whisper+gemma-hosted`** / **`gemini-fallback`** | **Cloud Transmission** | Spoken transcripts and visual frames are transmitted over HTTPS to Google AI Studio. Under the **Google AI Studio Free Tier terms**, submitted prompts, responses, and media may be reviewed by human annotators and used to train Google products. For private data, users must use local mode or a paid Google Cloud Vertex AI tier with data logging disabled. |

---

## 6. Living Heritage Principles
- **Respect for Cultural Sovereignty**: Traditional recipes and crafts belong to the communities and practitioners who maintain them. Craft Keeper is an open-source tool for documentation and community archiving, not for commercial exploitation or cultural appropriation.
- **Open Access**: Generated guides are exported in open formats (standard JSON and Markdown) with zero proprietary lock-in.
